The short version

This is a one-person business. I collect the minimum needed to answer your enquiry and run the site, I don't sell anything to anyone, and there's no advertising network involved.

If you contact me, I keep your message and details so I can reply and — if we work together — so I have a record of the engagement. That's essentially the whole story. The rest of this page is the detail the law requires.

What I collect, and why

When you use the contact form

  • Required: your name, email address and the description of your project.
  • Optional: company, country, project type, timeline, budget range and an existing site URL.
  • Automatic: the date and time of submission, and the IP address the submission came from — kept only as a spam-prevention measure.

This is used to reply to you, to prepare a quote if one is wanted, and to keep a record of the exchange. Nothing else.

When you book a call

Scheduling runs through [SCHEDULING PROVIDER], which collects your name, email, chosen time and timezone in order to create the appointment and send confirmations. Their privacy notice governs what they do with it: [LINK TO PROVIDER'S PRIVACY POLICY].

When you simply read the site

Standard server logs record IP address, browser type, referring page and pages viewed. These are generated by the hosting provider for security and troubleshooting, and are not used to build any profile of you.

Legal basis (UK & EU visitors)

If you're in the UK or the European Economic Area, the UK GDPR and EU GDPR apply. The bases I rely on:

  • Legitimate interests — replying to an enquiry you sent me, keeping the site secure, and preventing spam. You asked me a question; answering it is what you'd reasonably expect.
  • Performance of a contract — where we go on to work together, for everything needed to deliver and invoice the work.
  • Legal obligation — retaining invoices and tax records for as long as the law requires.
  • Consent — only if analytics or any non-essential cookies are ever added, and only after you've agreed.

Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, or delete it — and I'll do it without argument. If you're in the UK or EEA, these are enforceable rights rather than courtesies:

  • Access — a copy of what I hold about you.
  • Rectification — correction of anything inaccurate.
  • Erasure — deletion, where I have no overriding legal reason to keep it.
  • Restriction and objection — including objecting to anything I do on the basis of legitimate interests.
  • Portability — a machine-readable copy of what you provided.
  • Complaint — to your national supervisory authority. In the UK that's the Information Commissioner's Office.

Email vikas.bh14@gmail.com and I'll respond within 30 days, usually far sooner.

Security

The site runs over HTTPS. Access to the systems holding your data is protected by strong, unique credentials and two-factor authentication where the provider supports it. Software is kept current.

No system is perfectly secure, and I won't claim otherwise. What I can say is that the volume of personal data here is deliberately small — the less that's collected, the less there is to lose.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses and isn't intended for anyone under 16. I don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, email me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If this notice changes materially, the date at the top of the page changes with it. There's no version archive; if you need to know what it said on a particular date, ask.
