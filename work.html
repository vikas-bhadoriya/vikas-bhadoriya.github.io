Building for markets that ask harder questions

Roughly two thirds of this work was delivered for businesses outside India, across nine countries and four regulatory environments. That shapes how a site gets built long before design begins.

A Swiss medical device manufacturer has content that must survive review. A DIFC-regulated investment firm operates under communication rules that determine page structure, not just wording. A German agency needs consent handling that satisfies a market with particular expectations. An alcohol retailer needs age verification that doesn't destroy conversion.

These are not plugin decisions. They are architecture decisions, and getting them wrong is expensive in ways a redesign cannot fix.
